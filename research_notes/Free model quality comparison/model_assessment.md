# Free OpenCode Zen Models vs Claude Opus 5.5

_Research date: 24 September 2026. “Free” below means a currently listed OpenCode Zen route with zero token pricing; it does not imply a permanent free entitlement._

## 1. What is each model, and what is actually verified?

### Takeaway

The eight entries are not eight equally documented public models. Six are Zen aliases for named upstream families (Xiaomi MiMo, Meta Muse Spark, InclusionAI Ling, and NVIDIA Nemotron), while Big Pickle and Space Bunny are explicitly described by OpenCode as stealth/provider-specific routes. Claude Opus 5.5 is the best-documented reference point: Anthropic publishes its identity, limits, pricing, safety behavior, and benchmark results, and Artificial Analysis provides an independent current cross-model signal. The free Zen alias must not automatically be treated as identical to the canonical upstream checkpoint: Zen publishes different route limits for several aliases and says some free routes collect or permit use of data. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode Zen live model list](https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/models) [OpenCode-maintained model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)

### Cited Findings

**Catalog and reference point**

- OpenCode says Zen is a curated set of models that its team tested, configured with providers, and benchmarked as model/provider combinations. The documentation does **not** publish a per-model Zen quality table or the underlying benchmark scores, so “tested by OpenCode” is not a reproducible public quality claim by itself. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- The live Zen model-list endpoint includes the eight free route IDs relevant here: `space-bunny-free`, `mimo-v2.6-flash-free`, `muse-spark-1.3-contributor-free`, `ling-3.0-flash-fin-free`, `nemotron-3.5-lightning-free`, `muse-spark-1.2-contributor-free`, `nemotron-3-ultra-free`, and `big-pickle`. [OpenCode Zen live model list](https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/models)
- Anthropic’s current model overview describes Claude Opus 5.5 as intended for long-running agentic coding and knowledge work; it has a 1M-token context window, 128K maximum output, adaptive reasoning that is always on, and text/image input with tool use. Its published Zen price is $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. [Claude models overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview) [OpenCode Zen pricing](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- Anthropic’s launch results report Opus 5.5 as leading its tested agentic-coding, computer-use, and knowledge-work comparisons, including 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, 54.4% on FrontierCode v1.1, 1846 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1, and 67.7% with tools on Humanity’s Last Exam. Those are Anthropic’s reported results under its stated effort, safeguard, and fallback conditions, not a universal score for every OpenCode route. [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5)

### Inferences

- The most defensible interpretation is “canonical upstream family plus a particular Zen deployment,” not “the upstream model specification automatically equals the free endpoint.” The different MiMo/Nemotron context limits, contributor naming, and provider-specific entries support that interpretation. [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode) [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- Evidence quality is uneven: Opus 5.5, Muse Spark 1.3, and the two Nemotron models have both creator documentation and an independent Artificial Analysis entry; MiMo and Ling have strong creator documentation but their headline comparisons are largely vendor-reported; Big Pickle and Space Bunny have almost no quality evidence. [Artificial Analysis leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models) [Xiaomi model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL) [InclusionAI model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin) [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- Privacy is route-dependent. OpenCode says Space Bunny’s provider uses zero retention/no training, while it says data from Big Pickle, MiMo-V2.6-Flash, and Ling may be used to improve the models, NVIDIA free endpoints are trial endpoints with logging, and Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor Free permits prompts/completions to train future Meta models. Anthropic’s direct Opus page says zero data retention, while Zen’s privacy section says Anthropic API requests are retained for 30 days; direct Anthropic access and access through Zen should not be conflated. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5)

### Gaps

- I found no public creator, technical report, weights, parameter count, or reproducible benchmark for Big Pickle or Space Bunny; the available evidence establishes only their Zen IDs, route metadata, and OpenCode’s privacy/stealth descriptions. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)
- I found no OpenCode-published head-to-head scores for these eight free routes versus Opus 5.5. OpenCode describes internal testing and benchmarking but does not expose the per-model results. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- The current public Artificial Analysis pages I located do not have a model page for MiMo-V2.6-**Flash**. Its public page is for MiMo-V2.6-**Pro**, which is a different checkpoint; the Pro score must not be substituted for Flash. [Artificial Analysis: MiMo-V2.6-Pro](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/mimo-v2-6-pro) [Xiaomi Flash model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)
- Public model cards often describe a canonical checkpoint while Zen may serve a different quantization, context cap, or provider route. The practical quality of the exact free endpoint therefore remains partly unverified. [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode) [NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b)

## 2. Strengths, weaknesses, task fit, and evidence quality

### Takeaway

The strongest broad free candidates in the available evidence are **Muse Spark 1.3** (strong independent evidence, multimodal/agentic) and **MiMo-V2.6-Flash** (strong public technical evidence and self-reported coding/agent results, but no current independent Flash score). **Nemotron 3 Ultra** is a credible open-weight, long-context option; **Ling 3.0 Flash Fin** is a specialist finance model; **Nemotron 3.5 Lightning** is a speed/workhorse model; **Muse Spark 1.2** is capable but older and deprecated. Big Pickle and Space Bunny should be treated as unranked experiments, not assigned a quality tier from their names or catalog limits. [Artificial Analysis leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models) [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

### Cited Findings

#### Claude Opus 5.5 — reference model

- **Verified capability and fit:** Anthropic positions Opus 5.5 for long-running agentic coding and knowledge work, with 1M context, 128K output, adaptive reasoning, tool use, and image input. Its launch benchmark table reports 66.4% Terminal-Bench 4.0, 54.4% FrontierCode, 57.8% CursorBench, 1846 GDPval-AA Elo, 40.0% AutomationBench, 67.7% HLE with tools, 81.8% OSWorld 2.0 partial, and 89.0% Chartography with tools. [Claude models overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview) [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5)
- **Independent cross-check:** Artificial Analysis places Opus 5.5 at the top of its current Intelligence Index with 58 at max effort/default fallback. Its methodology weights agents 30%, coding 20%, scientific reasoning 20%, and general work 30%; it is primarily text/English and warns that the index is not a universal measure. [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking)
- **Tradeoffs:** Opus is paid, and Anthropic’s reported results include production safeguards and fallback behavior. Artificial Analysis also describes the model as very verbose in its evaluation workload. These are quality/efficiency caveats, not evidence that the free models are better overall. [OpenCode Zen pricing](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5)

#### Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor Free

- **Verified strengths:** Meta’s primary Muse Spark 1.1 announcement describes the family as multimodal and reasoning-oriented, with major improvements in tool/computer use, coding, multimodal understanding, million-token context management, multi-agent orchestration, and long-running computer-use workflows. Those are family-level claims, not a direct 1.3 benchmark. [Meta Muse Spark 1.1 announcement](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api/)
- The model catalog says 1.3 improves long-horizon agent collaboration, instruction following, and coding efficiency relative to 1.2; the weights are closed. [OpenCode model catalog: Muse Spark 1.3](https://models.dev/models/meta/muse-spark-1.3)
- **Independent evidence:** Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index of 48 for Muse Spark 1.3 max, 222.3 output tokens/second, 1M context, text/image/video input, proprietary weights, and very high verbosity. Its page describes the model as well above the comparable-price-class median, though not at Opus 5.5’s reported 58. [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3)
- **Caveat:** the free route is a contributor route, and OpenCode says its prompts/completions may be used to train future Meta models. The independent AA result is for Meta’s API/model page, not a direct test of the Zen contributor endpoint. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3)

#### MiMo-V2.6-Flash Free

- **Verified strengths:** Xiaomi’s official Flash-RL card describes native text/image/video/audio input, a 1M context, sparse-MoE execution, mixed reinforcement learning across coding/general-agent/visual/cybersecurity environments, and an agentic-grading/self-improvement loop. These are unusually concrete architecture/training claims, but they remain Xiaomi’s own description. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)
- **Vendor-reported performance:** in Xiaomi’s own table, Flash scores 67.9 on DeepSWE v1.1, 26.0 on ProgramBench, 61.2 on MiMo Code Bench, 73.6 on Toolathlon-Verified, 28.8 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and 80.8 on OSWorld-Verified. The same table reports Claude Opus 5 at 74.0, 37.0, 68.6, 80.6, 49.0, and 83.4 respectively; Flash is also reported at 52.3 on AutomationBench versus Opus 5’s 50.3. This is a mixed profile—competitive on some automation/visual tasks, behind Opus 5 on several coding/terminal tasks—and the comparison is not to Opus 5.5. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)
- **Caveats:** the Zen route advertises a 200K/32K context/output envelope rather than the canonical 1M/131K figures, and OpenCode says free-period data may be used for model improvement. The exact route’s modality exposure and tool behavior are not independently documented. [OpenCode model catalog: MiMo-V2.6-Flash](https://models.dev/models/xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash) [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Ling 3.0 Flash Fin Free

- **Verified strengths:** InclusionAI’s official card says Ling-3.0-flash-Fin was developed with financial institutions/domain experts and targets end-to-end financial research, authoritative-source search, multi-document reconciliation, valuation/spreadsheet workflows, and research-ready outputs. The card lists 124B total/5.1B active parameters, 256K context, and MIT weights. [InclusionAI Ling-3.0-flash-Fin model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin)
- The card reports evaluation across FinFIRST, FinSearchComp Verified, FinCRAFT, Finance Agent, APEX-Agents, SpreadsheetBench, and τ³-Banking, and claims particular strength in source selection and tool-intensive financial tasks. The public text does not expose a complete numeric comparison table, so the strength claim is vendor evidence rather than an independently reproduced result. [InclusionAI Ling-3.0-flash-Fin model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin)
- **Independent evidence and limits:** Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index of 23, 158.5 output tokens/second, 1.71-second time to first token, text-only input, 262K context, and very high verbosity. InclusionAI itself warns that complex long-horizon workflows still need further validation and that valuation/investment conclusions require professional review. [Artificial Analysis: Ling-3.0-flash-Fin](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/ling-3-0-flash-fin) [InclusionAI Ling-3.0-flash-Fin model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin)
- **Caveat:** OpenCode says Ling free-period data may be used to improve the model. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Nemotron 3.5 Lightning Free

- **Verified strengths:** NVIDIA describes Lightning as a fast 30B-total/3B-active hybrid Mamba/MoE model, intended for long-running autonomous agents, sub-agent workhorse deployments, and agentic workflows, with up to 1M context and multilingual/code support. [NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Vendor-reported results:** NVIDIA reports, for the NVFP4 checkpoint, MMLU Pro 81.62, GPQA Diamond 75.57, SWE-bench Verified 52.80, Terminal-Bench 2.1 23.46, GDPval-AA 865, IFBench 72.88, and AA-LCR 49.19. These are NVIDIA’s release-evaluation results, not a direct Opus 5.5 comparison. [NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Independent evidence:** Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index of 13, 264.9 output tokens/second, and 0.60-second time to first token. It describes Lightning as fast and above average among comparable open-weight models, while also noting verbosity and text-only input. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nemotron-3-5-lightning)
- **Caveat:** OpenCode says the NVIDIA free endpoints are trial use, log sessions for security/product improvement, and should not receive personal or confidential data. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Muse Spark 1.2 Free

- **Verified strengths:** the model catalog describes 1.2 as a coding-focused update to 1.1 with improvements in code generation, complex debugging, codebase understanding, and end-to-end developer workflows; it is a closed 1M-context model with tool, reasoning, structured-output, and temperature support. [OpenCode model catalog: Muse Spark 1.2](https://models.dev/models/meta/muse-spark-1.2)
- **Independent evidence:** Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index of 40 for Muse Spark 1.2 xhigh, 200.8 output tokens/second, 14.78-second time to first token, 1M context, and text/image/speech/video input. The same page marks the model deprecated and says 1.3 is the recommended successor; it also warns that non-default workload results are historical. [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-2)
- **Caveat:** the exact 1.2 free route is exposed in the live API/catalog but is not named as explicitly as 1.3 in the rendered Zen free/privacy prose. Its contributor/data terms should be confirmed before use. [OpenCode live model list](https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/models) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode) [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Nemotron 3 Ultra Free

- **Verified strengths:** NVIDIA describes Ultra as a frontier-scale, open-weight 550B-total/55B-active model for complex agentic workflows, long-context analysis, high-accuracy code/math/science reasoning, tool use, multilingual work, and high-stakes RAG. [NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b)
- **Vendor-reported results:** NVIDIA reports, for the NVFP4 checkpoint, Terminal-Bench 2.1 53.9, SWE-bench Verified 69.7, GDPval 47.9, GPQA 87.9, BrowseComp 41.4, IFBench 82.3, and AA-LCR 65.5. These figures support real strength in long-context/agentic work, but they are vendor results and are not a direct comparison with Opus 5.5. [NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b)
- **Independent evidence and operational caveat:** Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index of 23, 122.6 output tokens/second, and 2.50-second time to first token. NVIDIA’s own deployment guidance says the canonical model is extremely large and recommends multi-node hardware; a hosted Zen route hides that infrastructure burden but may expose a smaller context envelope and different serving behavior. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b) [NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)
- **Caveat:** OpenCode labels the free NVIDIA endpoints trial-only and says session data is logged for security and product/service improvement. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Big Pickle

- **What is verified:** OpenCode says Big Pickle is a limited-time stealth model used to collect feedback and improve it. The catalog supplies only provider-specific route metadata: 200K context, 32K output, reasoning, tools, structured output, and temperature control. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)
- **What is not verified:** no upstream lab, architecture, weights, parameter count, public benchmark, or independent quality comparison was found. It is therefore impossible to substantiate claims such as “better coder,” “Opus-level,” or “fast” from public evidence. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Artificial Analysis leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models)
- **Privacy caveat:** OpenCode says collected Big Pickle data may be used to improve the model during the free period. [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

#### Space Bunny Free

- **What is verified:** OpenCode says Space Bunny is a limited-time stealth model, while its provider follows zero retention and does not use submitted data for training. The catalog advertises a 1,048,576-token context, 524,288-token output, reasoning, and tools, but no upstream identity or quality evidence. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)
- **What is not verified:** I found no public model card, weights, benchmark, or independent evaluation that would support a quality ranking. The unusually large advertised context is a route capability, not evidence of reliable long-context reasoning. [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode) [Artificial Analysis leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models)

### Inferences

- **Best broad free substitute:** Muse Spark 1.3 is the most evidence-backed broad alternative because it has an independent index result, multimodal capability, and a creator-described agentic/coding focus. MiMo Flash is a co-leading candidate when coding, long context, and upstream openness matter, but its strongest numerical claims are self-reported and its Zen route is capped more tightly. [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3) [Xiaomi model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL) [OpenCode model catalog](https://models.dev/providers/opencode)
- **Best older/open-weight general workhorse:** Nemotron 3 Ultra has the strongest public NVIDIA evidence for long-context agentic work, but its independent broad index is far below Opus 5.5’s and its canonical deployment is unusually heavy. Muse Spark 1.2 remains potentially useful for coding, but its deprecation makes 1.3 the safer default when both routes are available. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b) [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-2) [NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b)
- **Best finance specialist:** Ling Fin is more plausibly useful than its general-purpose tier suggests for source-grounded financial research, valuation, and spreadsheet workflows. It should not be treated as a general Opus replacement; InclusionAI itself requires further validation and professional review. [InclusionAI Ling model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin) [Artificial Analysis: Ling-3.0-flash-Fin](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/ling-3-0-flash-fin)
- **Best speed-first choice:** Nemotron Lightning is the clearest candidate for high-volume subagents, shell/tool loops, and latency-sensitive batch work. Its AA speed signal is excellent, but its independent quality index is materially below the other named alternatives. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nemotron-3-5-lightning) [NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Stealth routes:** Big Pickle and Space Bunny should be selected only through a private bake-off. Big Pickle carries an explicit data-improvement caveat; Space Bunny’s stated zero-retention policy is a privacy advantage, but privacy does not establish coding or reasoning quality. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- **Opus’s practical role:** use Opus 5.5 for high-stakes, ambiguous, multi-file, or long-running work when its cost and route terms are acceptable; use the free models for exploration, parallel drafts, specialized finance, high-volume subtasks, and fallback behavior. This is a task-allocation inference from the documented capability differences, not a claim that every Opus setting beats every free route on every task. [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5)

### Gaps

- There is no controlled public test of all eight **exact Zen endpoints** with the same OpenCode agent harness, reasoning effort, tool schemas, context length, and retry policy. This prevents a defensible universal ranking. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Artificial Analysis coding-agent methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/agents/coding-agents)
- Vendor benchmark claims are not all comparable: MiMo’s table uses Claude Opus 5 rather than Opus 5.5, NVIDIA’s pages report their own harness/checkpoint settings, and Ling’s public card describes a benchmark suite without a complete text-readable numeric table. **Practical tiers**

1. **Tier 0 — reference frontier: Claude Opus 5.5.** Best-supported choice for difficult, ambiguous, long-running coding/knowledge work when cost is acceptable. Anthropic reports leadership across agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work; AA independently ranks it first among its current models. [Claude Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5)
2. **Tier A — closest credible free substitutes: Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor Free and MiMo-V2.6-Flash Free.** Muse 1.3 has the better independent broad evidence; MiMo has the more detailed public technical story and strong self-reported coding/agent results, but no current independent Flash index. ### Inferences

- If a single broad-quality ordering is necessary, use **Opus 5.5 > Muse Spark 1.3 ≈ MiMo-V2.6-Flash > Muse Spark 1.2 / Nemotron 3 Ultra > Nemotron 3.5 Lightning**, while treating Ling as a domain-specific exception and the stealth routes as unknown. The `≈` is deliberate: the evidence does not justify a precise MiMo-versus-Muse ordering, and AA/NVIDIA/vendor scores are not all comparable. [Artificial Analysis methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking) [Xiaomi model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL) [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3)
- The best choice can invert by task: Ling may beat a general model on finance retrieval/modeling; Lightning may be preferable for a high-volume subtask despite lower broad quality; Space Bunny may be preferable when the published zero-retention policy matters more than unknown quality. [InclusionAI model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin) [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nemotron-3-5-lightning) [OpenCode Zen privacy section](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- A practical bake-off should use the same OpenCode agent, representative repository tasks, tool-call success, patch/test pass rate, long-context retrieval checks, latency, token use, refusal/error rate, and privacy constraints. This recommendation follows from the documented differences in model purpose and from AA’s warning that benchmark results vary by task, settings, and provider.
