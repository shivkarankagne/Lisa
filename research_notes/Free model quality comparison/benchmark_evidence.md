# Eight OpenCode Zen free models vs Claude Opus 5.5 — benchmark evidence (September 24, 2026)

## What does the evidence say about each model?

### Takeaway

Claude Opus 5.5 is the strongest verified reference point in this comparison, but there is no fair, universal ranking of the eight Zen “free” entries. Several are time-limited aliases, contributor tiers, or trial endpoints; many published scores are for the underlying model or a paid first-party API rather than the exact free Zen route. The most defensible statement is therefore capability-by-capability, with source and configuration labels. [Anthropic: Introducing Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5) [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)

### Cited Findings

- OpenCode says Zen tests model/provider combinations, works with providers to ensure correct serving, and benchmarks the combinations it recommends; however, the public Zen documentation does not publish a per-model benchmark table or the exact free-route harness/results. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- The live Zen model endpoint fetched on September 24, 2026 contains the requested IDs (plus other free entries): `space-bunny-free`, `mimo-v2.6-flash-free`, `muse-spark-1.3-contributor-free`, `muse-spark-1.2-contributor-free`, `ling-3.0-flash-fin-free`, `nemotron-3.5-lightning-free`, `nemotron-3-ultra-free`, and `big-pickle`. [Zen `/v1/models` endpoint](https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/models)
- The Zen prose lists Big Pickle, Space Bunny, MiMo-V2.6-Flash, Ling 3.0 Flash Fin, Nemotron 3 Ultra, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, and Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor as limited-time free models, but does not mention Muse Spark 1.2 Contributor Free even though the live endpoint and model metadata list that ID. This is a documentation/catalog inconsistency that should be disclosed rather than silently resolved. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Zen `/v1/models` endpoint](https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/models)
- Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 launch table reports, under its stated evaluation conditions, Terminal-Bench 4.0 **66.4%**, FrontierCode v1.1 **54.4%**, CursorBench 4.0 **57.8%**, GDPval-AA v2.1 **1846 Elo**, AutomationBench **40.0%**, Humanity’s Last Exam **67.7% with tools**, Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 **58.7%**, OSWorld 2.0 **81.8% partial**, and Chartography **89.0% with tools**. Anthropic says most Claude results use adaptive thinking at max effort; Terminal-Bench 4.0 uses xhigh, and production safeguards can reduce some cyber/biology/frontier-task scores. [Anthropic: Introducing Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)
- Artificial Analysis independently reports Opus 5.5 at **58** on its Intelligence Index v4.3.2, with **59.6% Terminal-Bench 4.0**, **61.4% Humanity’s Last Exam**, **66.9% SciCode**, **1822 AA-Briefcase Elo**, and **1846 GDPval-AA v2.1**. Its model page lists a 1M context window, text-and-image input, text output, and reasoning; the page does not provide a comparable output-speed figure. [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5) [Artificial Analysis Opus 5.5 release analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-opus-5-5)
- Anthropic says Opus 5.5 generates output more than 30% faster than Opus 5, but gives no absolute Zen-route latency in the launch page. [Anthropic: Introducing Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)

### Inferences

- The only broadly comparable independent numeric baseline found for several models is Artificial Analysis’s displayed Intelligence Index; it is a composite of ten evaluations, not a universal measure of coding quality, and AA model pages show different effort/provider configurations. [Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5)
- “Free” does not mean “same as the public model”: Zen’s free IDs can impose smaller context/output limits, use contributor or trial data terms, or route to a different provider configuration. Those differences must be reported separately from the underlying model’s published capabilities. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Pi model metadata for Space Bunny Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/space-bunny-free)

### Gaps

- No public OpenCode report gives all eight free routes the same benchmark battery, no controlled Opus 5.5 head-to-head run, or confidence intervals for the free aliases; Zen’s public documentation describes testing but publishes pricing, privacy, and model IDs rather than per-alias scores. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- Free-route latency, queueing, rate limits, tool-call success rate, and multimodal acceptance were not published by Zen; the model metadata pages expose interface limits and compatibility flags, not task-success measurements. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [Pi: Space Bunny Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/space-bunny-free)
- Several aliases are explicitly described as limited-time or stealth offerings, so historical identity guesses or old user reports are not reliable evidence of the current underlying checkpoint. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/) [OpenCode GitHub issue #4276](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/issues/4276)

### Model-by-model evidence

#### Space Bunny Free

- **What is verified:** Zen lists Space Bunny Free as a limited-time stealth model; its provider is described as zero-retention and not using submitted data for model training. [OpenCode Zen documentation](https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/)
- **Interface metadata:** The OpenCode/Pi catalog advertises text and image input, reasoning enabled, 1,048,576-token context, 524,288 maximum output tokens, streaming, and tool-call compatibility. These are interface/capability claims, not task-success measurements. [Pi: Space Bunny Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/space-bunny-free)
- **Related but not proven identical model:** OpenRouter’s `stealth/space-bunny-alpha` page calls that anonymous model a large, fast, multimodal coding model with adjustable reasoning, 1M context, and text/image/video input. The page does not establish that the OpenRouter Alpha ID and OpenCode’s `space-bunny-free` alias are the same checkpoint; treat the video and “strong coding” descriptions as unverified for the Zen route. [OpenRouter: Space Bunny Alpha](https://openrouter.ai/stealth/space-bunny-alpha)
- **Benchmarks:** No official model card, parameter count, independent benchmark score, or reproducible Opus 5.5 comparison was found for Space Bunny Free. “Strong coding” and “blazing-fast” are provider/marketing descriptions, not benchmark results. [OpenRouter: Space Bunny Alpha](https://openrouter.ai/stealth/space-bunny-alpha)

**Inference:** Space Bunny Free has an unusually broad advertised interface (reasoning, tools, 1M context, image input), but its actual quality relative to Opus 5.5 is currently **unrankable**, not proven near-Opus. [Pi: Space Bunny Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/space-bunny-free)

#### MiMo-V2.6-Flash Free

- **Underlying model:** Xiaomi describes MiMo-V2.6-Flash as a sparse MoE reasoning model with **309B total / 15B active parameters**, **1M context**, and text/image/video/audio input; the public model page lists tool calls, web search, structured output, streaming, and 128K maximum output. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash](https://mimo.mi.com/models/en-US/mimo-v2.6-flash) [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)
- **Official benchmark evidence:** Xiaomi’s model card reports MiMo-V2.6-Flash versus **Claude Opus 5**, not Opus 5.5: DeepSWE v1.1 **67.9 vs 74.0**, ProgramBench **26.0 vs 37.0**, MiMo Code Bench **61.2 vs 68.6**, Toolathlon-Verified **73.6 vs 80.6**, Terminal-Bench 4.0 **28.8 vs 49.0**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 **87.6 vs 89.1**, OSWorld-Verified **80.8 vs 83.4**, JobBench **61.2 vs 65.7**, and MiMo VisualCoding **71.5 vs 70.0**. The table also reports AutomationBench **52.3 vs 50.3** and Agents’ Last Exam **27.6 vs 31.6**. These are Xiaomi’s reported evaluations and are directional rather than a controlled Opus 5.5 comparison. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)
- **Zen alias caveat:** The OpenCode/Pi metadata for `mimo-v2.6-flash-free` advertises only **200,000 context** and **32,000 maximum output tokens**, even though the underlying Xiaomi model advertises 1M/128K. The alias metadata also exposes text and image input, not the full audio/video surface shown on Xiaomi’s underlying-model page. [Pi: MiMo-V2.6-Flash Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/mimo-v2-6-flash-free) [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash](https://mimo.mi.com/models/en-US/mimo-v2.6-flash)
- **Independent benchmark gap:** No Artificial Analysis model page for MiMo-V2.6-Flash was found in the sources retrieved, so there is no verified independent composite score to place beside Opus 5.5. The available official comparison is to Opus 5, not Opus 5.5. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL)

**Inference:** MiMo-V2.6-Flash is a credible high-capability open model, but the vendor table places it below Opus 5 on most of the listed coding, terminal, and tool-use tests, with some exceptions in automation and visual coding. The free Zen route’s smaller advertised context makes it a less direct substitute for Opus 5.5’s 1M context. [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL model card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL) [Pi: MiMo-V2.6-Flash Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/mimo-v2-6-flash-free)

#### Muse Spark 1.3 Free (Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor Free)

- **Official capability claims:** Meta says Muse Spark 1.3 improves agentic and coding performance, is designed for longer-horizon work and tool use, preserves more constraints across multi-step tasks, and asks for clarification when needed. In Meta engineer comparisons with 1.2, Meta reports approximately **20% fewer tool calls** and **25% fewer tokens**; this is a vendor comparison against the preceding Muse version, not an independent Opus 5.5 test. [Meta: Introducing Muse Spark 1.3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)
- **Official interface:** Meta’s Model API documentation describes parallel tool calls, streamed tool arguments, reasoning carried across turns, and a 1,048,576-token context window. [Meta Model API overview](https://ai.developer.meta.com/docs/overview/)
- **Free-alias metadata:** Zen’s `muse-spark-1.3-contributor-free` entry advertises text/image input, reasoning, 1,048,576 context, and 131,072 maximum output tokens at $0. [Pi: Muse Spark 1.3 Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/muse-spark-1-3-contributor-free)
- **Independent evidence:** Artificial Analysis measures Muse Spark 1.3 (max) at **48** on its Intelligence Index, **222.3 output tokens/s**, and **23.09 seconds time to first token**, with text/image/video input and 1M context. [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3)
- **Benchmark-image limitation:** Meta’s release page embeds a scorecard image comparing Muse Spark 1.3, 1.2, GPT-5.6 Sol, and Opus 5, but the individual cells were not available as text in the retrieved page. No numeric cells should be transcribed from the image without a separate verified extraction. [Meta: Introducing Muse Spark 1.3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)

**Inference:** Muse Spark 1.3 Free is the closest free model to Opus 5.5 in the independent composite evidence retrieved (48 versus Opus 5.5’s 58), but that is not proof that the contributor-free route has identical quality to the paid max configuration. [Artificial Analysis: Muse Spark 1.3](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-spark-1-3) [Artificial Analysis: Claude Opus 5.5](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5)

#### Ling 3.0 Flash Fin Free

- **Underlying model and specialization:** InclusionAI describes Ling-3.0-flash-Fin as a finance-enhanced continuation of Ling-3.0-Flash, with **124B total / 5.1B active parameters**, 256K context, source-grounded financial search, multi-document financial reasoning, valuation/spreadsheet workflows, and tool-intensive financial research. The model card says it still needs further validation in complex long-horizon workflows. [InclusionAI Ling-3.0-flash-Fin model card](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash-Fin)
- **Independent composite results:** Artificial Analysis reports **23** on the Intelligence Index, **24** on Finance & Accounting, **1171 GDPval-AA v2 Elo**, **967 AA-Briefcase Elo**, **7% AutomationBench-AA**, and **0% Terminal-Bench 4.0**. It also reports about 67K output tokens per Intelligence Index task. [Artificial Analysis: Ling-3.0-flash-Fin](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/ling-3-0-flash-fin) [Artificial Analysis Ling release analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/ant-group-releases-finance-focused-ling-3-0-flash-fin)
- **Latency and context:** Artificial Analysis measures approximately **158.5 output tokens/s** and **1.71 seconds TTFT** on InclusionAI’s API, with 262K context and text-only input. These are not Zen-free-route measurements. [Artificial Analysis: Ling-3.0-flash-Fin](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/ling-3-0-flash-fin)
- **Zen alias metadata:** The free alias advertises text-only input, reasoning, 262,144 context, 32,768 output, and tool-call support. [Pi: Ling 3.0 Flash Fin Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/ling-3-0-flash-fin-free)

**Inference:** Ling 3.0 Flash Fin Free may be useful for finance research/spreadsheet workflows, but the independent 0% Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 23 composite score make it unsuitable as an evidence-backed general coding replacement for Opus 5.5. [Artificial Analysis: Ling-3.0-flash-Fin](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/ling-3-0-flash-fin)

#### Nemotron 3.5 Lightning Free

- **Underlying model:** NVIDIA describes Nemotron 3.5 Lightning as a 30B-total/3B-active hybrid Mamba/MoE model, text-only, with up to 1M context, optimized for long-running autonomous agents and sub-agent execution. [NVIDIA Build: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Official release evaluation:** The NVFP4 evaluation table reports MMLU-Pro **81.62**, GPQA Diamond **75.57**, HLE text-only **10.47**, SciCode **31.38**, SWE-bench Verified **52.80**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 **23.46**, PinchBench **83.43**, BrowseComp **36.81**, τ³-Banking **9.48**, GDPval-AA-V2 **865**, IFBench **72.88**, and AA-LCR **49.19**. NVIDIA says these are release evaluations with reproducible recipes. [NVIDIA Build: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Independent results:** Artificial Analysis reports **13** on the Intelligence Index, **264.9 output tokens/s**, and **0.60 seconds TTFT**, with 1M context, text-only input, and 31.6B total/3.6B active parameters. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nemotron-3-5-lightning)
- **Latency/product positioning:** NVIDIA says the model reaches up to four times the output speed of similar-sized models and is intended for high-volume execution, validation, and routine tool calls; NVIDIA’s blog reports 86% PinchBench, while the model card reports 83.43 for the NVFP4 checkpoint. The discrepancy should be retained as a source/version caveat rather than averaged. [NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-lightning-delivers-fast-accurate-specialized-task-execution-for-long-running-agents/) [NVIDIA Build: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b)
- **Zen alias caveat:** The free alias metadata advertises 262,144 context and 262,144 maximum output, despite the underlying NVIDIA model page advertising up to 1M context. [Pi: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning Free](https://pi.dev/models/opencode/nemotron-3-5-lightning-free)

**Inference:** Lightning’s strongest evidence is speed and high-volume agent execution, not frontier capability. Its independent composite score of 13 is far below Opus 5.5’s 58, and its official Terminal-Bench 2.1 result is not a controlled comparison with Opus 5.5’s Terminal-Bench 4.0 result. [Artificial Analysis: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/nemotron-3-5-lightning) [Anthropic: Introducing Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)

## How do the free models compare with Claude Opus 5.5?

### Takeaway

Among models with a current Artificial Analysis composite score, Muse Spark 1.3 is closest to Opus 5.5 (48 vs 58), followed by Muse Spark 1.2 on the current page (40), while Ling 3.0 Flash Fin and Nemotron 3 Ultra each show 23 and Nemotron 3.5 Lightning shows 13. MiMo has strong official benchmark data but no retrieved independent composite score; Space Bunny and Big Pickle have no verifiable quality score. This is a comparison of displayed model/provider results, not proof that the free Zen routes preserve the same performance.
