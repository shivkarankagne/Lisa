## 1. Why this package exists

The user added a real rent agreement — a phone-scanned stamp-paper PDF
and Word drafts — asked "what was the monthly rent?", and got "I could
not find this in your documents." in 0.3 s. Neither file was readable
(scanned PDFs had no text layer; `.docx` was not supported), so the
collection was empty and the similarity floor answered without running
the model. The user moved `.docx` and OCR from plan §8 L3 into 1.0
(decision 16, Apple Vision for OCR).

## 3. Measurements

M2, Release build.

- **Vision OCR:** 0.4–0.65 s per page at 2,000 px. On the user's stamp
  paper every English line was right, including "monthly rent of Rs.
  2,000/- (Rupees Two Thousand only)". Vision has no Hindi or Telugu:
  the stamp's Devanagari lines are lost (L3: Tesseract).
- **User's own files** (`~/Documents`, `~/Downloads`, read locally, in a
  scratch directory): 158 files read (4,935 chunks), 8 without text
  (drawings), 1 password-protected, in 20.5 min, peak RSS 2.5 GB. Nine
  questions: 8 of 8 answerable ones answered correctly with citations
  (books, pitch deck, resume, interview notes, legal petition, rent
  agreement); the off-topic control declined. 6–10 s per answer.
- **Rent question** on the scan plus the Word draft: "Rs. 2,000 [1]…
  document [3] states Rs. 3000. The documents differ."

## 4. Behaviour changes found by real documents

- **Similarity floor 0.40 → 0.25** (`LISA_ASK_OPTIONS_INIT`, W8). The
  0.40 floor was calibrated on short one-fact passages (report 009). Real
  chunks (~1,000–1,500 characters, several clauses) score lower: the
  notice-period clause scored 0.33 and every chunk fell under 0.40, so
  LISA said "not found" without asking the model. At 0.25 the question
  is answered; off-topic questions are still stopped before the model
  when nothing is even loosely related, and otherwise declined by the
  model (layer 2).
- **Conflicting sources:** the prompt now tells the model to give each
  answer with its citation and say they differ (was: picked the Word
  draft's Rs. 3000 over the signed scan's Rs. 2,000).
- **Settings route** identifies a model file by exact name and size
  instead of hashing it in the request (hashing 0.6–2.5 GB stalled the
  request; found by the sanitizer run). `lisa model` still verifies the
  full SHA-256.
- `lisa search --json` reports each hit's similarity.
